# User understanding

*Made-up content for the Requisio demo.*

## Who uses Requisio
| User | Who they are | How often | Comfort with software |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Requester** | Any employee who needs to buy something | A few times a month | Mixed |
| **Approver** | Team leads and finance | Daily | Medium |
| **Admin** | Procurement team | Weekly | High |

## Needs
- Requesters: raise a request **quickly**, know **where it is** and **when** it will be decided.
- Approvers: see **what is waiting** for them, decide **in one place**, see **why** something costs what it does.
- Admins: set **who approves what**, by department and amount, without asking IT.

## Pain points today
- Requests are sent by email and get lost.
- Nobody knows who still has to approve.
- Rules ("over 5,000 needs Finance") live in people's heads.

## Design notes
- Simple words, few steps, clear status everywhere.
